How I work

Relational, collaborative, and adapted to you rather than the other way round.

In our work together, we begin to make sense of what's been going on in a way that feels manageable and meaningful.

That might involve exploring current challenges, noticing patterns in relationships, or understanding experiences that have shaped how you relate to yourself and others. We go at a pace that feels right, and nothing is forced.

An integrative approach

As an integrative counsellor, I draw from a range of approaches including person-centred, psychodynamic and elements of CBT, alongside existential, systemic, narrative and solution-focused perspectives.

In practice that means some sessions look like making sense of the present, others like understanding where a pattern began, and others like working out what you actually want to do next. My work is relational and collaborative rather than advice-giving or purely solution-focused.

I adapt how I work to suit you, rather than expecting you to fit into a particular approach.

What a session is like

Fifty minutes, weekly

Usually at the same time each week, which helps the work build. Short-term and longer-term work are both fine.

Online, on Teams

Video or phone, via Microsoft Teams. Somewhere you can speak freely matters more than anything technical.

Led by you

You bring what you want to bring. If you don't know where to start, that's a perfectly good place to start.

Identity, culture and context

My practice is informed by an awareness of identity, culture, power and lived experience. I understand that our difficulties do not exist in isolation, and I aim to offer a space where important parts of who you are can be explored without judgement or the need to minimise your experience.

For some people that's central to why they come. For others it rarely comes up. Both are fine. What matters is that there's room for it.

The easiest way to find out if this fits is a short conversation.
