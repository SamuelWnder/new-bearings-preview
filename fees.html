Fees and questions

Clear pricing, and answers to the things people usually want to know before getting in touch.

£65 per 50-minute session
  • Free 15-minute introductory call, with no pressure to continue
  • Weekly sessions, online via Microsoft Teams
  • Short-term and longer-term work both welcome
  • A limited number of reduced-fee spaces, including concessions for keyworkers and students

If cost is the thing standing in the way, please ask. Availability changes, and I would rather have the conversation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if therapy is right for me?

You don't need to be certain. That's what the free 15-minute call is for. You can ask whatever you like, and there's no expectation that you book anything afterwards.

What if I don't know where to start?

That's extremely common, and it's a perfectly good place to begin. You don't need a tidy explanation of the problem before you arrive.

How long will I need to come?

It varies. Some people come for a handful of sessions with something specific in mind. Others stay longer and go deeper. We'll talk about what suits you, and revisit it as we go.

What if it isn't working?

Then I'd want to know. You're free to decide whether therapy with me is right for you, and you can stop at any point if it stops being helpful. That isn't a failure and it won't offend me.

Who do you work with?

Adults, individually, and a limited number of couples while I complete a Post-Qualifying Certificate in Relational Counselling. Sessions are online, so I work with people right across the UK. Family work will follow as my Systemic Family Therapy training progresses.

Is what I say confidential?

Yes, with the usual limits I'll explain when we start, which relate to risk of serious harm. I work under regular clinical supervision, where client work is discussed anonymously. I'm registered with the ICO and fully insured.

How quickly will you reply?

I aim to respond within 24 hours.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

Not at the moment. I work online, which means we can meet wherever you are. In-person sessions may be offered in future.

Still wondering whether it's worth getting in touch? It usually is.
