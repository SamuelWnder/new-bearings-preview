About me

I'm Fatuma Hassan, an integrative counsellor working online with adults across the UK.

Starting therapy can feel like a big step, especially if you're used to managing things on your own. It's important that you find someone you feel comfortable with.

I offer a warm, thoughtful and non-judgemental space where you can speak openly, feel heard and begin to make sense of what's going on. My way of working is calm, down-to-earth and relational, and I'll meet you at a pace that feels right for you.

I believe therapy should be a space where you don't have to hold everything together.

Many of the people who contact me are carrying more than others realise. They may be navigating relationship difficulties, major life changes, workplace pressures, competing family responsibilities, or questions about identity and belonging. Often they have spent a long time adapting to changing circumstances, meeting expectations, solving problems for others, or simply doing what was necessary to keep going.

Therapy offers an opportunity to slow down, make sense of what is happening, and explore what you need.

“You don't need to have everything figured out before reaching out.”

Free 15-minute introductory call. Sessions 50 minutes, online via Microsoft Teams. I aim to reply within 24 hours.

What I bring to the room

Before becoming a therapist, I worked across corporate, charity and healthcare-related settings in consultancy, knowledge management and project-based roles. I also hold a Master's degree in Business Analysis and Consultancy.

Those experiences gave me insight into workplace pressure, responsibility, change and the challenges of balancing competing demands. They continue to inform my understanding of many of the difficulties people bring to therapy, particularly around stress, identity, work and life transitions.

As a parent, I also understand many of the realities that come with balancing work, family life and caring responsibilities. Speaking multiple languages and having lived and worked across different cultural contexts has given me additional perspectives on identity, belonging, family expectations, relationships and navigating different worlds.

Rather than focusing solely on symptoms, I am interested in understanding the wider picture: your relationships, personal history, values, cultural context, life experiences and the meanings you make of them.

My aim is not to tell you who you should be or what decisions you should make. Instead, I offer a space where you can understand yourself more deeply, reconnect with what matters to you, and find a way forward that feels right for you.

Experience

Alongside private practice, I have worked with adults, children and young people across a range of settings, including local authority services, voluntary organisations and workplace counselling services.

My experience includes supporting people experiencing anxiety, stress, relationship difficulties, identity concerns, discrimination, workplace challenges and life transitions.
