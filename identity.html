Identity & sense of self

Belonging, values and understanding who you are.

There are times in life when we begin to question who we are, where we belong, or whether we are living according to our own values.

Sometimes this follows a significant life event. Sometimes it develops gradually over time. You may feel caught between different expectations, communities, cultures or roles. You may have spent so long adapting to meet the needs of others that you are no longer sure what feels right for you.

Questions about identity can affect relationships, confidence, decision-making, career choices and your overall sense of direction. Counselling can provide a space to explore these questions without pressure to arrive at quick answers.

Together we might explore:

Identity and belongingCulture and heritageFamily expectationsValues and meaningFaith and spiritualityMigration and adaptationSelf-worth and confidencePurpose and direction

Speaking multiple languages and having experience of different cultural contexts has shown me that identity is rarely simple. We are often shaped by many different influences, relationships and experiences.

Therapy can provide space to understand those influences more clearly and develop a stronger sense of who you are becoming.

The privilege of a lifetime is to become who you truly are.

Carl Jung

If any of that sounds like where you are, that is enough to start a conversation.
