Life transitions

Navigating change without losing yourself.

Life does not always unfold in the way we expect. Sometimes change arrives gradually. Other times it can feel as though life has shifted overnight.

You may be:

Becoming a parentParenting through different stages of family lifeChanging careersMoving home or countryExperiencing bereavementEnding a relationshipAdjusting to a health diagnosisSupporting an ageing parent or family memberTaking on caring responsibilitiesNavigating changes in family rolesWondering what comes next

Even positive change can bring uncertainty. You may find yourself wondering who you are now, how much responsibility you can carry, what happens when the roles in your family begin to change, or how you support others without losing yourself.

Transitions often ask us to let go of old certainties before new ones have fully emerged. Therapy can offer a space to pause, understand what is changing, and explore how to move forward whilst staying connected to yourself and what matters most.

When the map no longer works, it may be time to find new bearings.

If any of that sounds like where you are, that is enough to start a conversation.
