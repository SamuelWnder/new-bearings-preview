Pressure, responsibility & overwhelm

When you're carrying too much.

Many of the people I work with are the people others depend upon. They are often parents, partners, professionals, carers, adult children, siblings, friends and helpers.

From the outside they may appear to be coping. Inside they may feel exhausted.

You may recognise:

Constant overthinkingDifficulty switching offFeeling responsible for everyone elseSelf-criticismPerfectionismParenting pressuresCaring for others while holding yourself togetherWork pressure and competing demandsBurnout and emotional exhaustionFeeling guilty when prioritising yourself

Sometimes the pressure comes from work. Sometimes it comes from parenting, caring responsibilities or supporting people you love through difficult periods. Whatever its source, carrying too much for too long can leave you exhausted, disconnected and unsure how to keep going in the way you have been.

Therapy offers a place to put some of that weight down. Together we can explore what you are carrying, where those expectations come from, and how you might create more space for yourself whilst remaining connected to the people who matter.

Having worked in demanding professional environments and experienced the realities of balancing work, family life and competing responsibilities, I recognise many of the pressures people find themselves navigating.

Sometimes strength looks less like carrying on and more like putting something down.

If any of that sounds like where you are, that is enough to start a conversation.
