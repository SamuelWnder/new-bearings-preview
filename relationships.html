Relationships

Understanding patterns, connection and change.

Relationships can be one of the greatest sources of comfort in our lives, but also one of the greatest sources of stress and pain.

You may find yourself having the same arguments repeatedly, struggling to communicate your needs, feeling disconnected from those closest to you, or wondering why similar patterns keep appearing in different relationships.

You may find yourself:

Putting other people's needs firstAvoiding conflictStruggling with boundariesFeeling responsible for keeping relationships goingFeeling lonely or misunderstoodUnsure how to communicate what you need

These difficulties can affect confidence, wellbeing, family life and the way we see ourselves.

In counselling, we can explore the patterns that may be contributing to these difficulties and consider where they came from, how they developed and whether they are still serving you.

Together we might explore:

Romantic relationshipsFamily relationshipsFriendshipsAttachment and connectionCommunication difficultiesBoundariesPeople-pleasingTrust and intimacyRepeating patternsThe influence of family, culture and expectations

My training in relational counselling and systemic family therapy reflects a particular interest in how relationships shape our lives and how change becomes possible within them.

If any of that sounds like where you are, that is enough to start a conversation.
